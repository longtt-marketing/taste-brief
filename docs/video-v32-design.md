# Product film revision — 27 October 2026

The owner rejected v31's visual quality. Its full-page stills, small interface text and long static holds were unsuitable for a product film. This revision restores the visual approach of the earlier Claude-designed film while retaining the current decision desk and verified claims.

Two directions were inspected locally: dark studio with large type and focused UI crops, and a cream editorial comparison. The dark studio direction was selected for continuity with the earlier film. Claude Design was opened in Chrome Work but displayed a usage limit until 28 October at 15:00; it did not produce a new design. UI/UX Pro Max informed typography, focus and motion. The built-in image generation tool supplied the new illustrative studio background. No supporting agent was used.

The film is 1920×1080 at 30 fps, approximately 125 seconds. It retains five seconds of the earlier v28 illustrative opening, then uses eleven new scenes: the price question, buyer inputs, actual Qloo shortlist, assumed limits, package arithmetic, changed price, delivery threshold, unknown real terms, historical evidence, negative comparison and a closing invitation. Interface crops come from the recorded 27 October coffee run. Explanatory graphics do not pretend to be continuous app execution. Invented quote terms are labelled; the 65%/52% historical result and agent21/popularity25 result retain their limitations.

The renderer is `scripts/build-motion-video.py`, with narration in `docs/demo-motion-v32.json`. Local assets, scene proofs, the alternate direction, original score, speech timings and editable outputs are retained in ignored `video/v32/`. Rendering requires Pillow, NumPy, edge-tts and FFmpeg. The five-second opening, generated studio and font are tracked in `design/video-v32/`; UI crops use the existing `public/shots/decision-*.png` files. Run prepare, stills, render, audio and assemble in that order. Instrument Sans is used under its SIL Open Font License, retained beside the local font. The ambient score and transition sound are synthesized by the renderer.

Validation: all eleven scene proofs inspected, full MP4 decode passed, H.264/AAC streams verified, captions generated from word timings. A full-range/limited-range mismatch between the legacy intro and new body was found in the final encoded frame and fixed before release. The opening was cut before the previous narration begins. Body loudness measured -16.34 LUFS with true peak -1.45 dBTP. Final asset hashes, public byte parity, video URL and Devpost verification are in `video-v32-release.json`.

Image-generation prompt (built-in tool, no API fallback): cinematic 16:9 podcast studio, premium broadcast microphone, espresso cup and headphones on a smoked oak desk at right; deep ink navy shadows, subtle teal rim light and a warm amber practical light; left half quiet for white typography; realistic materials, restrained film grading; no people, text, logos, screens, watermark or floating decoration.
